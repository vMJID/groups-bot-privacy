Privacy Policy – Groups Bot

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Groups Bot processes, stores, and protects data when used within Discord servers.

1. Information We Process

Groups Bot may process Discord-related information required to provide its functionality, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Role IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Group membership information
  • Group ownership and leadership information
  • Group activity statistics
  • Message activity points
  • Voice activity and voice duration statistics
  • Group rankings and related statistics
  • Bot activity and system logs

Groups Bot does not intentionally store the actual content of users' messages in its database.

2. How We Use Data

The information processed by Groups Bot is used only to provide and maintain its functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing groups
  • Creating and managing group roles and channels
  • Managing group members
  • Calculating group activity points and rankings
  • Tracking message activity and voice participation
  • Protecting group roles and channels
  • Processing designated blocked-role actions
  • Maintaining system and administrative logs
  • Managing group-related resources

3. Message Content

Groups Bot uses Discord message content when necessary to process its traditional message-based commands and calculate message activity points.

Message content is processed for these purposes but is not intentionally stored as message content in the bot's database.

Groups Bot does not use message content to train, develop, or improve artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Data Storage

Certain Discord-related data is stored outside of Discord on the server hosting the bot. The stored information is limited to data necessary for the bot's functionality.

Stored data is protected using appropriate security measures, including encryption at rest.

5. Data Retention

Different categories of data may have different retention periods depending on their purpose. Temporary operational and protection records may be automatically deleted after their applicable retention period.

Certain system and audit logs may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, auditing, and maintaining the bot's functionality.

6. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of activity data associated with them by contacting the bot owner through Discord. Deletion requests may be subject to verification.

7. Data Sharing

Groups Bot does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be accessed by the bot owner or authorized administrators when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or troubleshoot the bot.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Stored data is encrypted at rest.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data processing practices change. The latest version will be made available on this page.

10. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding data processed by Groups Bot, users may contact the bot owner through Discord.
